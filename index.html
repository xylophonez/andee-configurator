Configure your node

Set up your phone, then choose your AI in the extension.

  1. Add your wallet below. Copy its public address from the PermawebOS extension.
  2. Load and restart. Download the configuration, open Load JSON in PermawebOS, then Apply & close and reopen.
  3. Connect and chat. Paste your node's public link into the extension, then choose free AI, ChatGPT or an API provider.

Profile

Applied layers

    Your agent

    Your agent, AI connections, messaging and your node's execution tools are configured together.

    Unlock the extension and copy your wallet address at the top. Use that same wallet for ChatGPT sign-in. No API key or callback URL is needed here.

    Choose an AI provider

    Free AI is provided by APUS. Your messages are sent to their servers.

    Leave blank to discover models automatically and choose in chat. To specify models, paste IDs one per line or separated by commas. The first is the default; prefixes such as ~ and suffixes such as :free are kept.

    The key stays in your private configuration. Previews and clipboard copies hide it.

    Tunnel configuration

    Connect from anywhere through a tunnel. After applying, reopen PermawebOS to see and share your node’s public URL.
